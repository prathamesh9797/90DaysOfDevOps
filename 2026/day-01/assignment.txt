    What is your understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?
    >>
    DevOps - DevOps = developers and operations people working together smoothly.
    Before DevOps:

Developers wrote code
Operations ran servers
They blamed each other when things broke 
With DevOps:
Everything is automated
Code moves from “written” → “live on the internet” quickly and safely
Fewer errors, faster updates

A DevOps engineer:
Automates deployments (no manual copying files)
Monitors systems so problems are caught early
Makes sure updates don’t break the app
Helps teams release new features faster
Real-life example:
A well-organized kitchen where chefs cook and the staff clean and restock at the same time—no chaos.

Cloud engineering = running computers on the internet instead of in your office.
Instead of buying and maintaining physical servers, you rent them from companies like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

A cloud engineer:
Sets up servers on the internet
Stores data safely online
Makes sure apps/websites are fast and available 24/7
Scales things up when more users show up (and down when they leave)
